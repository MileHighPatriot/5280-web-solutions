Questions

Good to know.

Will the brushes scratch my paint?

No. The tunnel uses closed-cell foam that can't hold grit, rinsed constantly while it spins. It's safe for clear coat, wraps and ceramic coatings.

Can my truck fit?

Up to 7 feet tall and 80 inches wide at the mirrors. Dually trucks, lift kits over 6 inches and open beds full of gear need the self-serve bay.

How does Unlimited work?

One plan covers one vehicle. Wash once a day, every day, at either location. It renews monthly and you can pause or cancel from your phone.

Do you close for weather?

We stay open in light snow. The status chip at the top of this page shows when the tunnel is closed.

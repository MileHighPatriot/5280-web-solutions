Framing & siding contractor · Denver, CO

Concept project

Helix Frame & Siding

A contractor site with an interactive design studio: homeowners repaint the siding, trim, and deck on a real house before asking for an estimate.

Visit the live site

The goal

Siding is a visual decision, and homeowners struggle to picture colors from a swatch. This concept turns the website into a sales tool: customers try colors on a real photo, and the estimate form picks up their choices.

Design decisions

Let customers play

A design studio repaints just the siding, gables, or trim on a real project photo. Sky, brick, and landscaping stay as photographed.

Estimates that carry context

The estimate form reuses the customer's design choices, so the first conversation starts with a clear scope.

Show the process

Blueprint and plan-sheet pages show the planning behind the work, which builds trust before the first call.

What’s built in

  • Interactive color and material design studio
  • Estimate, permit, and contact request forms
  • Service, project, and blueprint detail pages
  • Team, trade partner, and review pages
  • Fast static hosting

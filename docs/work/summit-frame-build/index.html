Framing contractor · Aurora, CO

Concept project

Summit Frame & Build

An editorial, magazine-style site for a family-run framing crew, built to make a trade business look as premium as its work.

Visit the live site

The goal

Most framing companies have a phone number and a few photos online. This concept shows how a small crew could stand out to custom-home builders and homeowners with a site that feels like a high-end design magazine and still gets straight to "request an estimate."

Design decisions

Lead with the work

A full-screen drone video of a house going up opens the site. Visitors see the craftsmanship before they read a word.

Editorial typography

A refined serif paired with a clean sans gives the crew a premium voice that stands apart from typical contractor sites.

Before & after proof

Case studies use a draggable before/after slider that works with a mouse, a finger, or the keyboard.

What’s built in

  • Full-bleed video hero with phone-sized version
  • Accessible before/after slider
  • Working contact form on a fully static site
  • Local SEO: structured data, sitemap, social share image
  • Motion that respects reduced-motion settings
